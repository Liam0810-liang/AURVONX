Factory history and local context

Guangdong Zhengutang Biotechnology Co., Ltd. was incorporated in 2023 according to public company information and operates in Qingyuan’s growing beauty-manufacturing cluster. In a 2025 report about Qingyuan’s “beauty economy”, the company’s quality and safety representative described receiving one-to-one guidance from the local cosmetics industry service station to organize filing materials and support a product launch.

This public report describes local industry support and the company’s participation in a filing process. It does not establish a universal production capacity, export count, customer count or product success rate.
