Less breakage versus less shedding: a crucial catalogue distinction

AURVONX · 14 September 2026

Breakage concerns the hair fibre. Shedding concerns release from the follicle. A grooming study that counts broken fibres cannot support a claim to prevent follicular hair loss.

Choose the right product formats

The core kit pairs a rinse-off mask with a choice of leave-on conditioner. The fuller-look scalp serum is a separate visual-density project and is not evidence of new hair growth.

Understand the ingredients

Exact protein identity and the conditioning base should be reviewed together. A caffeine or peptide headline must not be used to imply a proven regrowth mechanism for the finished serum.
