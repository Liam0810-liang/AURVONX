Curl gel, strong-hold gel and wet-look gel: write different briefs

AURVONX · 14 September 2026

A gel can target structured hold, curl definition or a glossy wet look. The product family should make that difference visible before the buyer reads the INCI list.

Choose the right product formats

Use a comparison set with controlled doses. For curl trials, record the leave-in product and drying method so a change in gel is not confused with a change in routine.

Understand the ingredients

The community gel-cast question illustrates why instructions matter. A visible cast is not a universal measure of success; the buyer may value softness, definition or volume differently.

Discovery set: choose a gel for the intended finish. Curl gel can be evaluated with a small amount of leave-in on separate tests.
