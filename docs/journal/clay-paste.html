Hair clay versus paste: communicate finish and reworkability

AURVONX · 14 September 2026

Matte clay, stronger texture clay and flexible paste should differ in grip, shine and reworkability. A numeric hold badge is only useful when its scale is defined.

Choose the right product formats

Package these as a barber or buyer selection set with a comparison card. The card asks about spreading, styling time, reworking and washing out.

Understand the ingredients

Wax, powder and film-former categories help organize the brief. They do not establish a precise hold level until the finished products are compared.

Build a useful collection

Discovery set: choose one clay or paste per style. Compare on separate uses rather than layering all three.

Plan your private-label range

Begin with a clear customer need and choose formats with distinct roles. Compare application preferences, packaging and the place of each product in the routine. Your destination market and sales channel should guide the final assortment.
