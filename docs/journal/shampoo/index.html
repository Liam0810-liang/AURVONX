Choosing a private-label shampoo: cleansing, feel and residue

AURVONX · 14 September 2026

A shampoo brief must describe the scalp cleansing target and the feel of the hair afterwards. More foam or a stronger cooling sensation is not automatically better performance.

Choose the right product formats

Start with the smooth shampoo and light conditioner. The volume and scalp-fresh shampoos are alternatives for different briefs, rather than a three-shampoo daily regimen.

Understand the ingredients

Ask the lab to explain the surfactant blend and deposition system. Ingredient categories matter, but a single surfactant name does not describe how the final formula behaves.
