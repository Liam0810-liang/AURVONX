Volume powder versus dry shampoo: grip and oil appearance are different

AURVONX · 14 September 2026

A volume powder adds styling grip, while dry shampoo aims to refresh oily-root appearance between washes. Neither should be described as replacing scalp washing indefinitely.

Choose the right product formats

Keep the powder and aerosol dry shampoo as alternatives. The kit needs a clear dose card and should not encourage dusting all products onto the scalp together.

Understand the ingredients

Absorbent ingredient categories guide the brief, but particle properties, dispensing and actual use determine exposure and residue. Review the complete format with the safety assessor.
