Barrier-care formulation: why the whole cream matters

AURVONX · 14 September 2026

A barrier-care range should put cleansing comfort and moisturizer performance ahead of an extensive active checklist. Texture, repeated use and tolerance are part of the brief.

Choose the right product formats

A milk cleanser, serum and cream create distinct jobs. Offer the gel cream or fluid lotion when the buyer needs a lighter texture; avoid presenting all three moisturizers as necessary.

Understand the ingredients

Dexpanthenol and ectoin each have formulation-specific human studies. Ceramide content alone does not prove an optimized barrier-lipid system. Request ingredient identity and the complete emulsion brief.

Barrier lipids · Panthenol (Vitamin B5) · Botanical comfort ingredients · Humectants · Conditioning systems · Hair oils and emollients · Ectoin

  • Vehicle-controlled ectoin trial · 2007Primary clinical study

Build a useful collection

Gentle milk cleanser, B5 serum and ceramide cream. Cica gel cream and ectoin lotion offer alternative textures.
