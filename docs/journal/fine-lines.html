Retinol, retinal or peptides: designing a focused night-care range

AURVONX · 14 September 2026

A fine-line collection can offer a comfortable peptide-led routine and a separate retinoid option. Putting retinol and retinal into one mandatory layering routine makes the assortment harder to understand.

Choose the right product formats

The base kit is serum plus cream, with eye cream as an optional area-specific step. The retinoid extension needs its own introduction instructions and suitability review.

Understand the ingredients

The retinoid guidance explains why tolerance and gradual introduction matter. Peptides are a broad ingredient family: request the exact INCI and supplier evidence before describing a mechanism.
