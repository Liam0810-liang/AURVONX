Hair oil or leave-in milk: choose by the job, not the name

AURVONX · 14 September 2026

A hair oil can be an oil blend or a formulated finishing product. Its commercial name does not reveal whether it contains silicones, esters or botanical oils.

Choose the right product formats

This set is a finishing discovery kit: compare the oil with the ends serum on separate styling occasions. They are alternatives, not a requirement to coat the hair twice.

Understand the ingredients

Research comparing particular oils does not justify transferring coconut-oil findings to argan, camellia or squalane. The formula and application method must match the claim.
