Scalp serum: moisture care is a distinct product brief

AURVONX · 14 September 2026

A scalp serum can focus on dry-feeling skin and comfortable application without making hair-growth claims. The applicator and residue profile are important parts of this brief.

Choose the right product formats

Offer two alternative leave-on textures and one shampoo. Do not automatically combine the exfoliating scalp essence with both leave-on serums.

Understand the ingredients

Skin ingredient studies are useful context but do not by themselves establish scalp performance. Investigate contact time, hair-parting application and interaction with styling residues.
