LEARN BEFORE YOU ORDER

Answers buyers search for

What is AURVONX?

AURVONX is the beauty brand of Guangzhou Huanyue Cross-border E-commerce Co., Ltd. Huanyue coordinates private-label beauty projects with manufacturing partners in Guangdong, China.

Where are AURVONX projects coordinated and manufactured?

Customer communication and project coordination are handled by Guangzhou Huanyue in Guangzhou. Guangdong Zhengutang Biotechnology Co., Ltd. is the named manufacturing partner in Qingyuan, Guangdong. A product is considered for production only after its scope and feasibility are confirmed with the partner.

Does “75 product types” mean 75 formulas?

No. The 75 refers to product types in the catalogue. Each type can be discussed across formula direction, ingredient choices, texture, fragrance and packaging. Exact formula availability and specifications are confirmed for the selected SKU; the count is not a claim of 75 finished formulas.

Which categories can I discuss?

Skincare, haircare and styling are our core strengths. The catalogue spans routines and formats in those areas. Other beauty and personal-care categories can be evaluated with the manufacturing partner against the market brief and confirmed scope.

What should I send to get a useful project response?

Share your destination market, product type or catalogue code, target customer, estimated order quantity, target price position and packaging preference. Reference products or a draft brand brief help us identify open questions before sampling.

Are the certificates shown current and applicable to every product?

No blanket claim is made. The certificates displayed in the quality library list validity through 15 May 2026 and should be treated as historical until current copies are confirmed. Certification scope, validity and market requirements must be checked for the selected manufacturing partner and project.
