CASE FORMAT

How a first collection becomes easier to sell.

Brief: a new e-commerce brand wants a skincare hero product with a clear routine extension.

Decision: start with one brightening serum, one barrier cream and one hydration set; keep the first order within the channel’s cash-flow plan.

Execution: align claim language, texture, pack, content angles and replenishment trigger before production.

Learning: sales feedback determines the next SKU instead of adding a large range before product-market fit.

FORMULA DIRECTION

A product type is a starting point, rather than a single fixed formula. Discuss active ingredients, texture, fragrance, colour, performance objectives and target cost.
